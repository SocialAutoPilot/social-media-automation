## Why Start With 5 Accounts?

Social media automation is often presented as something designed for huge operations.

It doesn't have to be.

You might start with:

- 1 Facebook account
- 1 Instagram account
- 1 X account
- 1 YouTube channel
- 1 TikTok account

Then expand as your publishing workflow becomes more useful.

For a small business, creator, marketer, or agency, the first challenge isn't managing thousands of accounts.

It's figuring out **what should be automated, what content should be published, and how consistently you can execute it.**

That's where a smaller free setup makes sense.
